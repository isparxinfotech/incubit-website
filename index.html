Who we are

An operator, not a fund

Every Incubit operation sits in an emerging market, and that is deliberate: these are the places where energy demand is still growing, and where a mid-sized operator can build real businesses with real assets. We run what we own. The group is backed by institutional equity and debt investors in Singapore and the Middle East, invests without a fund clock, and operates on a simple principle: capital and strategy at the centre, operations with professional management in each business.

Principle

Hold like an owner

No exit horizon and no fund clock. Businesses are built and held for the long term, through cycles, in markets we understand.

Principle

Operate like an institution

Professional management teams run each business. The centre does three things: capital, strategy and standards.

Principle

Build where others aren't looking

Mid-sized energy businesses in growing markets, below the radar of global majors and above the reach of local players.

How we operate

Capital and strategy at the centre. Operators everywhere else.

Incubit is led by its founder and principal, Harsh Sinha. His role is strategy and capital allocation, not operations: each business is run by its own professional management team, held to institutional standards of governance and reporting. It is a structure built for compounding, not for control.

What the centre does

Allocates capital. Sets direction. Holds every business to one standard of governance, safety and reporting. Nothing else.

What the businesses do

Everything else. Pricing, people, plants, customers: decisions sit with the teams closest to them, in the markets where they live.

Perspective

"In five years there will be no such thing as an energy company that is not a technology company. And that shift will happen in emerging markets first."

The view we build by

The data that matters in energy is physical: it sits in tanks, on forecourts and in blending plants, not on the internet. Asset owners are best placed to use it, and markets without legacy systems will use it first. That conviction shapes how every Incubit business is being built.
